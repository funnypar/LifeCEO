Five departments, one CEO

Each tab looks after one part of your life. Together they tell you what's next, what's due and what's left.

Today

Reminders that won't let you forget.

  • A full-screen alarm that rings on the lock screen and through silent mode
  • Done, or Snooze for 10 minutes. Ignore it and you get a "Missed" note
  • Your day as a sky arc, with a live countdown to the next reminder

Subs

Know before you're charged.

  • Warnings before renewals, and before free trials turn into paid plans
  • Monthly and yearly totals, plus a 30-day renewal strip
  • A spending breakdown by category

Projects

See how far along you are.

  • Progress in 10% steps. Tap the bar to update
  • Deadline countdowns and the average across all projects
  • A gentle hint when a project has been idle for a week

Ideas

Every idea gets a decision.

  • A weekly board meeting: Fund it into a project, Hold it, or Kill it
  • A wildcard round that merges two old ideas into a new pitch
  • Catch ideas from a Quick Settings tile, with an optional voice memo

Money

Your monthly plan at a glance.

  • Salary, bills and subscriptions give you what's free this month
  • Needs / wants / savings vs. 50/30/20, and rent vs. a 30% limit
  • Bill reminders, and exchange rates you control

Install in a minute

Life CEO isn't on the Play Store. You install the APK file directly.

  1. Download LifeCEO.apk on your Android phone.
  2. Open the file from your notifications or the Files / Downloads app.
  3. If Android blocks "unknown apps", tap Settings, turn on Allow from this source, and go back.
  4. Tap Install, then Open.
  5. Allow the permissions the app asks for, so your alarms ring on time.
Play Protect warning? Google may flag apps from outside the Play Store. Tap More details → Install anyway.
Updating: install the new APK over the old one. Your data stays. Don't uninstall first, because that deletes your data. Coming from Daily? Install over it and everything carries over.

Verify the download (optional): each release lists the SHA-256. Compare it with shasum -a 256 LifeCEO.apk.

FAQ

My alarm didn't ring.

Open Settings → Check setup and allow every item. Some phones (Xiaomi, Huawei, Samsung, Oppo…) also have their own battery saver. Set Life CEO to "Unrestricted" there.

Does it ring in silent mode?

Yes. It uses the alarm volume, like your clock app.

Is there an iPhone version?

No, Android only (Android 12 or newer).

Can I move my data to a new phone?

Not yet. Data lives on the phone only.

Why isn't it on the Play Store?

It's a small personal project. Sideloading keeps it free and simple.

What changed recently?

See the changelog. The latest version renamed Daily to Life CEO.
