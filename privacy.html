Privacy Policy

Effective 26 November 2026

The short version
  • Everything you enter stays on your phone.
  • The app only goes online if you turn on the optional assistant (Android, off by default), and only when you use it.
  • No account, no ads, no analytics, no tracking.
  • We never sell or share your data, and the assistant's server keeps none of what you send.

This policy explains how the Life CEO app for Android and iPhone ("the app") handles your information. It covers the Google Play version, the Android version downloaded from this website, and the App Store version for iPhone. The app is made by MOPANO, an independent developer ("we").

Information the app stores

To work, the app saves what you type into it:

  • Reminders and alarms (titles, times, repeat rules)
  • Subscriptions and bills (names, amounts, due dates)
  • Money planning (salary, monthly plan, spending entries)
  • Projects and tasks
  • Ideas, their notes, board meeting minutes, and any voice memos you record
  • App settings such as theme and clock format

This information is saved only in the app's private storage on your device. Other apps cannot read it, and it is never uploaded to us or to anyone else, except for what the optional assistant sends while you use it (see below).

Information we collect

None, unless you use the assistant. The app contains no analytics, crash reporting, advertising or tracking code, and it does not ask for your name, email, location, contacts, photos or any account. The iPhone app never connects to the internet.

Juno, the assistant (Android, optional)

From version 5.0 the Android app has an assistant: you say or type one thing ("remind me to call Mom at 6pm") and it does it. It is off until you turn it on, and the app explains what it sends before you do. You can turn it off any time in Settings › Juno. From version 5.1 the assistant is called Juno, and you can rename it.

Each time you use it, the app sends over an encrypted connection:

  • what you said or typed, and the last few things said while the assistant is open;
  • a short summary of your lists: the names, times, amounts and dates of your reminders, subscriptions, projects, income and bills, and totals such as what's free this month. Notes, ideas, shopping lists and voice memos are never sent;
  • a random ID created when you turned the assistant on, used only to count requests (50 a day per phone);
  • the assistant's name and the reply style you picked (Concise, Friendly or Playful). The name you give for yourself so Juno can greet you is never sent.

This goes to our server on Cloudflare, which passes it to Cloudflare's AI service (Workers AI) to work out what you meant, and sends the answer back to your phone. To read Juno's reply out loud, the reply text is sent the same way to Cloudflare's text-to-speech and the sound is sent back (a copy of recent spoken lines is kept on your phone so they don't need to be fetched again). Nothing you send is saved or logged by us; Cloudflare handles it under Cloudflare's Privacy Policy. The only thing kept is a count of requests per random ID and per network address for the current day, deleted the next day.

Voice is turned into text by your phone's own speech recognizer (on the phone where available, otherwise your phone's speech service), only while you hold the mic or orb, or after you tap it until you pause. The app sends only the resulting text. Spoken replies are read out by your phone's own text-to-speech voices, offline.

Voice memos are recorded only when you tap record on an idea. The audio file is saved in the app's private storage next to the idea and is deleted when you delete the idea. It is never uploaded or transcribed.

Device backup

If you have turned on Android's own backup to your Google account, Android may include the app's data in that backup so it can be restored on a new phone. This backup is run by Google under Google's Privacy Policy. We have no access to it. You can turn it off in your phone's Settings under Google › Backup.

On iPhone, if iCloud Backup is on, iOS may include the app's data in your iCloud backup so it can be restored on a new iPhone. This backup is run by Apple under Apple's Privacy Policy. We have no access to it. You can manage it in Settings › your name › iCloud › iCloud Backup.

Links that leave the app

The Settings screen has links to email, GitHub and Telegram. Tapping one opens that app or website, which has its own privacy policy. The app shares nothing with them; you choose what to send.

Deleting your data

Because your data lives only on your phone, you are in full control. To delete it on Android, uninstall the app, or go to Android Settings › Apps › Life CEO › Storage › Clear storage. On iPhone, delete the app: touch and hold its icon, then Remove App › Delete App.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and collects no information from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date. If a future version of the app ever starts collecting data, this policy will be updated before that version is released.
