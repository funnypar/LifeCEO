# Changelog

## v5.2 — Talk to Juno
- **Juno has its own button**, bottom-left, opposite the +. It's a little character now: a wobbly blob with eyes that blink and look around, and it ponders with thought bubbles while it works out an answer. The + is a plain add button again.
- **Tap Juno for a voice conversation.** It greets you, listens, answers out loud and listens again, hands-free. Tap it to interrupt; when you go quiet it dozes until you tap it. Hold Juno for a quick one-off ask.
- **A new voice**: a young British man. Juno's words appear as it says them, starting in about a second. Offline, it uses your phone's own voice.
- Juno greets you by name when it opens, and the Playful personality is properly playful now.
- **50 requests a day**, up from 10. A softer, lower listening chime.

## v5.1 — Meet Juno
- The assistant now has a name: **Juno, your chief of staff**. It has a face too, an animated gradient orb that reacts while it listens, thinks and speaks.
- Hold the + and just talk: the screen edges glow with your voice and your words show live. Swipe up on the + for the full Juno screen, where you can type.
- **Undo** or Edit anything Juno adds.
- **Make it yours** in Settings → Juno: rename it, tell it your name (never sent), pick one of four looks and a voice, and choose Concise, Friendly or Playful replies.
- Juno can read its replies out loud: when you talk, always, or never.
- Long-press the app icon for **Ask Juno**.

## v5.0 — Just say it
- **An assistant** (Android): tap the mic next to the gear on any tab and type, or hold it and talk. "Remind me to stretch at 7am on weekdays", "Netflix 12.99 a month, renews on the 3rd", "How much is free this month?"
- It adds, changes and deletes reminders, subscriptions, projects, income and bills, and answers questions about them. One thing at a time.
- New things are added straight away, with anything it guessed marked in amber and an Edit button. Changes and deletes wait for your Confirm.
- **Off until you turn it on.** It's free, with 10 requests a day. Only while you use it are your words and a short summary of your lists sent to work out what you mean; nothing is kept. Everything else stays on your phone.

## v4.7 — Your data, safe
- **Updates never lose your data.** If anything in your data can't be read, only that one item is left out; everything else loads, and the file as it was is kept safely on your phone, with a button to send it for recovery.
- Each update keeps a copy of your data from the version before.
- Data saved by a newer Life CEO is never overwritten by an older one.
- **Export and import** in Settings → Your data: everything, voice memos included, in one file. Use it to back up, to move to a new phone, or to switch between this download and the Google Play version.
- Android's own backup now includes your data and voice memos.

## v4.6 — Shopping
- A new **Shop** tab, right after Today. Type "milk @Lidl" to tag a store; the list is grouped by store, with chips to show one store at a time.
- Tick things off as you buy them. Everything you've bought becomes a **usual item** you can put back on the list in one tap.
- Add a quantity ("2 kg") to any item, and share the list as text with anyone.
- Each project gets its own **board**: To do, In progress and Done. Once a project has tasks, its progress follows them.
- **One-time income**: log a bonus, a gift or a freelance job. It counts toward that month only.
- Easier setup: the battery-limits step is gone. Reminders still ring on time.
- Rebuilt to share its code with the new iPhone version.

## v4.5 — Ideas
- A new **Ideas** tab. Every idea is a pitch to you, the CEO.
- A weekly **board meeting** (Sunday 19:00 by default) shows waiting pitches one by one: **Fund** turns it into a project, **Hold** brings it back next week (three holds and it's a final pitch), **Kill** sends it to the graveyard, where you can resurrect it.
- A **wildcard round** picks two old ideas and asks: what if these were one thing?
- Catch ideas fast from a Quick Settings tile or by long-pressing the app icon. Add a note or a voice memo of up to 2 minutes. Memos stay on your phone.
- Meeting minutes are saved, and the board meeting day and time can be changed in Settings.

## v4.4 — New icon

- Matching themed icon (Android 13+) and notification icon.

## v4.3 — Life CEO
