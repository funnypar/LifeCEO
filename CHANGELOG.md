# Changelog

## v4.3 — Life CEO
- The app has a new name: **Daily** is now **Life CEO**.
- Same app and same data. It installs over Daily and keeps everything.

## v4.2 — Settings
- A gear on every tab opens Settings.
- Appearance: System, Light or Dark. Time format: Automatic, 12-hour or 24-hour.
- "Check setup" re-opens the permissions screen. Money settings are one tap away.
- Contact (email, GitHub, Telegram) and an optional tip jar. Never any popups.

## v4.1 — Splash screen
- An interactive opening screen with the sky of the current hour and a rising sun.

## v4.0 — Money
- New Money tab: salary and recurring bills, with what's free this month after bills, subscriptions and savings.
- Needs / wants / savings against the 50/30/20 guideline, and rent against a 30% limit.
- Any home currency, including Toman and TL, with exchange rates you set yourself.
- Bill reminders.

## v3.1 — Spending
- Subscription categories and a Spending screen: this month, a 12-month chart, a category breakdown and the biggest renewals ahead.

## v3.0 — Daylight design
- A new look: a sky card that follows the time of day, soft cards, a color for each section, and a new icon and alarm screen.

## v2.1 — Projects
- New Projects tab: track progress in 10% steps, with optional deadlines and idle hints.

## v2.0 — Subscriptions
- New Subs tab: renewal and free-trial warnings, and monthly and yearly spend.

## v1.0 — First release
- Daily reminders with a full-screen alarm, Done / Snooze, and missed-reminder notifications.
